# #90 — Decide what of the Life app's offline working the console wants

Raised 2026-08-06, as a question rather than a plan: read what the Life app does offline and work out which of it belongs here — possibly none of it.

The console is not obviously the same case. Life is a record you add to and reconcile later; the console is a window onto processes running on a Mac that is either reachable or not, and there is nothing useful to do with a session you cannot reach. The parts that might carry over are the ones about the phone rather than the server: an unsent message kept (#89), a transcript readable while the tunnel is down, a queued send that goes when the connection returns.

Note against a service worker in particular: the console deliberately has none, because it would cache an app that sits behind a client-certificate gate, and ngsw's navigationUrls and auth are a known source of trouble here. Anything offline has to work without one, or that decision has to be reopened on purpose.
