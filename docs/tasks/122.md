# #122 — "answered" is drawn from the write, not the read — so a deaf session shows a green answered card and stays blocked

2026-08-08: `health` asked a single-select `AskUserQuestion` at 09:29:26. The answer was tapped at 09:30:44 and the session was still blocked 31 minutes later, `busy: "requesting"`, transcript frozen at 09:29:27, `toolu_01QsgrDMunyooNTbB4tSmS5P` unanswered.

**The card on the phone said `answered — Re-bless, file 05-15 as a defect`, in green.** Screenshotted over CDP; that is what made the diagnosis possible and what refuted the first one.

## The defect

`Session::decide` writes the decision line to the child's **stdin**, flushes, and only then pushes `Event::Answered`. The console draws the green *answered* state from that event. So *answered* means exactly what *waiting to be read* means one layer up: **the bytes reached the pipe.** It says nothing about the CLI having read them.

Against a session that has stopped reading stdin ([[reference_console_session_stops_reading_stdin]]), the console therefore reports the answer as delivered and accepted while the session sits blocked on the same question for ever. Worse than the *waiting* marker, which at least stays visibly pending — this one is affirmatively wrong.

⚠ **This was first filed as a swallowed tap in `pick()`.** That was wrong: the guards in `session-view.ts` never fired, the POST was made, and it reached the pipe. The client is not at fault. Corrected once the phone's own screen was read.

## What to build

1. **Draw *answered* from evidence that it was read.** The turn resuming — the transcript growing, the tool result landing, the session leaving `requesting` — is the receipt. Until then the card should read as *sent, not yet taken up*, the same state as a queued message.
2. **The same alarm as #121 covers this**, and needs to: a session in `requesting` with a decision written and no transcript growth is deaf, and today nothing says so. `busy: "requesting"` plus a frozen mtime is the whole test.
3. Keep the client-side hardening from the original filing as a smaller, separate point — `pick()`'s three silent bail-outs (`!entry.ask`, `allowed !== undefined`, `replying`) should each say why rather than no-op — but it is not what happened here and should not be described as a live bug without a reproduction.

## Why it matters more than it looks

This is the third deaf episode of 2026-08-08 and the **second session** — `hardware` at 08:47 and 09:37, `health` at 09:29. It is not one bad session. And unlike a queued message, a blocked question stops the session dead: `health` lost 31 minutes having already reported it could go no further without the answer.
